# Recovery Integrity

Covered by `tests/integration/test_backup_restore.py`,
`tests/security/test_storage_recovery.py` and the real S16 laboratory step.
The negative matrix includes corrupted payloads, missing payloads, malformed
manifests, occupied destinations, migration failures, crash-mid-ingest and
retention/evidence cases. The restore result is only accepted after checksum,
SQLite integrity and foreign-key validation.

The E2E evidence records backup directory, manifest and independent receipt
verification. Audit rows generated by backup/restore are treated as legitimate
append-only differences; business rows and evidence blobs must remain equal.
